Unique Opportunity - Partially Built Home on 5.04 Acres in Annaghdown, Just Outside Galway City

Looking to build your dream home without the hassle of securing planning permission? This partially built house in Annaghdown presents a rare opportunity to create a forever home in a tranquil rural setting - without the restrictions of the local need planning requirement.

Key Property Features:

Blockwork completed - ready for the next stage of construction

Spacious 5.04-acre site offering privacy and potential

Peaceful countryside location just a short drive from Galway City

Ruins of an old stone cottage adding history and character to the property

Planning permission granted avoiding the lengthy planning process

No local need restriction - open to buyers who wouldn't typically qualify for planning in the area

Planning & Development Highlights:

Full planning permission was granted for a 318 sqm serviced dwelling and garage/shed.

The property benefits from an established planning approval, meaning the new owner can proceed with the build without the usual local need requirements.

The house exterior must have a nap plaster or stone (grey) finish, ensuring it blends with the natural surroundings.

The roof must be blue/black, dark grey, or brown for visual harmony.

A traditional stone boundary wall is required, enhancing the rustic charm of the site.

The site is fully serviced, with conditions in place for surface water disposal and septic tank installation in line with EPA guidelines.

Why is This a Unique Opportunity?

Securing planning permission in rural Ireland can be difficult due to local need regulations, which typically require applicants to have family ties or an essential housing need in the area. This property eliminates that challenge, making it a turnkey opportunity for those who want to build in Annaghdown without those restrictions.
